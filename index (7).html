About Vision 6

What it is

Vision 6 turns a written description into a detailed image. Type what you want to see, pick an optional style, and generate.

Reference images

Upload your own image, or click any image in the gallery, to use it as a visual reference. Vision 6 will use it alongside your prompt to guide the result.

My creations

Every image you generate is saved to My creations for this session, so you can revisit or download it later.

Cost

Vision 6 is free to use. Image generation runs on your own free account, so there's no cost to you beyond signing in once.
